Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

YoVibe operates a platform for discovering events and venues, creating events, and buying and delivering tickets. This Privacy Policy explains how YoVibe collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects personal information. For privacy questions or requests, contact reinolmartin0001@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, account type, profile photo, and account settings.
  • Google Sign-In information: if you choose Google Sign-In, we use the basic profile information Google provides to create and secure your YoVibe account: your email address, display name, and profile photo when available.
  • Ticket and guest-purchase information: buyer and recipient details, event and ticket selections, seat or table selections, order references, payment status, and ticket validation records.
  • Payment-related information: payment references, payment method, amounts, and payment status. Payment processors handle payment credentials; YoVibe does not collect or store full card or mobile-money credentials.
  • Organizer and event information: venue, event, payout, staff, and content details submitted by organizers.
  • Optional media: security photos, profile images, and venue or event media that you or an organizer choose to upload.
  • Device and service information: notification tokens, basic device or browser information, and analytics information used to operate, secure, and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to operate the platform, create and secure accounts, process and confirm ticket purchases, deliver tickets and service messages, manage events and venue operations, prevent fraud and abuse, provide customer support, comply with legal obligations, and improve reliability and user experience. We use optional security photos only for the safety or entry-verification purpose communicated when they are collected.

Google user data

YoVibe requests only the basic Google profile data described above. We do not request, read, store, or use Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google account content. We do not sell Google user data. YoVibe does not use Google APIs to generate, alter, or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery.

When we share information

We share information only as needed to run YoVibe and deliver a requested service: with hosting, database, storage, analytics, payment, email, and notification providers; with the relevant event organizer or venue when needed to issue, manage, or validate a ticket; and with authorities or professional advisers where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including access controls and authenticated service integrations, to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security; please keep your account credentials confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized activity.

Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. You can request account deletion through Profile → Settings → Delete Account or by contacting us at the email above. After deletion, we remove or anonymize account information where practicable, while retaining only records needed for legal, payment, fraud-prevention, security, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may stop marketing or non-essential notifications through the relevant device or account settings where available. Some information is necessary for account security, ticket delivery, payment reconciliation, or legal compliance and may need to be retained as described above.

International processing and changes

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We will update this page when this policy changes and revise the effective date. Continued use after a change is subject to the updated policy.
